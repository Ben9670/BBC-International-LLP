About BBC International

BBC International is a professionally managed apparel sourcing and export company specializing in woven, knit, and denim categories. From vendor development and merchandising to inspections and logistics, we manage the entire supply chain with a strong focus on compliance and timely delivery.

Our network of audited factories and a dedicated on-ground team ensures that every order meets buyer specifications, international quality standards, and ethical practices.

Our Core Services

End-to-end solutions from product development to final shipment.

Sourcing

Vendor identification, fabric & trim sourcing, and factory allocation based on product category, price, and compliance.

Merchandising

TNA creation, sample follow-up, order confirmation, and day-to-day communication between buyer and factory.

Quality Inspections

Inline, midline and final inspections with detailed AQL-based reports to ensure shipment-ready quality.

Exports & Logistics

Coordination with freight forwarders, documentation support and ensuring on-time shipment and delivery.

Stocklots

Structured stocklot solutions with clear documentation, size ratio, and packing details for quick buying decisions.
