Our Services

End-to-end apparel sourcing, merchandising, quality and export solutions for global brands and retailers.

BBC International supports the complete lifecycle of an order – from initial product brief to final shipment. Our team manages communication, sampling, production follow-up, quality inspections and documentation, ensuring a smooth and transparent process for every buyer.

Core Service Areas

01. Sourcing

Identification and allocation of the right factory based on product category, price level, lead time and compliance requirements.

  • Vendor identification & onboarding
  • Fabric & trim sourcing
  • Price negotiation support
  • Factory capacity planning

02. Merchandising

Day-to-day coordination between buyer and factory, ensuring clarity at every stage from sampling to shipment.

  • TNA preparation and tracking
  • Sample development & approvals
  • Order confirmation follow-up
  • Daily production communication

03. Product Development

Assisting buyers with trend-right styles, fabric options and cost-effective constructions tailored to their market.

  • Design & style suggestions
  • Fabric / wash / trim proposals
  • Fit sample & size set coordination
  • Cost engineering & value additions

04. Quality & Inspections

Dedicated quality team conducting inline, midline and final inspections with detailed reporting.

  • PP meeting & risk analysis
  • Inline & midline inspections
  • Final AQL inspection & reports
  • Process audits when required

05. Logistics & Exports

Supporting documentation, coordination with freight forwarders and ensuring on-time dispatch of shipments.

  • Booking & vessel coordination
  • Shipping documentation support
  • Labelling & packing approvals
  • Follow-up until goods reach port

06. Stocklots

Structured stocklot solutions with clear documentation for quick, transparent buying decisions.

  • Verified stocklot listings
  • Size ratio & packing details
  • On-ground inspection support
  • Flexible MOQ and pricing
