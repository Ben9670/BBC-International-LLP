About BBC International

Who We Are

BBC International is a professionally managed apparel sourcing and export company, providing comprehensive solutions in sourcing, merchandising, inspections, logistics, and stocklots.

With over a decade of experience, we bridge the gap between buyers and compliant manufacturing partners across India. Our strengths lie in our deep vendor network, strong production follow-up systems, and our ability to deliver consistent quality across woven, knit, and denim categories.
